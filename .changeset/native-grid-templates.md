feat(layouts): native `Grid` / `Columns` by size class, mobile templates, and the Mac window (krizaka-ui#753, #741). Native: `Grid` picks its columns from the width it is given (compact < 600 pt, regular < 840, expanded) — never from a device name — with `minItemWidth` (grown by the text scale, so nothing overflows at 200 %), gap tokens, equal heights and right-to-left safety; `Columns` sit side by side or stack; `useSizeClass`, `useFontScale`. New templates on `Screen`: `ListDetail` (one pane on a phone, two when the room holds them), `FormFlow` (steps, pinned action), `StateScreen` and `OfflineBanner` (empty, error, offline), a dashboard and a settings home; `Screen` gains `onRefresh` (pull to refresh). Web: `DesktopWindow` (`@krizaka/ui/desktop-window`), the window of a Tauri Mac app — sidebar · content · inspector, resizable by pointer and keyboard, collapsible, a toolbar that moves the window and clears the traffic lights, the desktop's density and the macOS focus ring — and `desktopMenus` / `useDesktopCommands`, which turn the window's commands into the menu bar's menus (`setNativeMenu` of `@krizaka/app-desktop`), handlers and the page's shortcuts. A « Frames, layouts and templates » page on ui.krizaka.com says which to use at which size class.
