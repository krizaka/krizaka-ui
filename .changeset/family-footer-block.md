`FamilyFooter` (`@krizaka/ui/site-footer`): the footer of the developer sites as one block, built on `SiteFooter`. It takes `footerFor(siteId)` of `@krizaka/org` ≥ 1.4.0 as a prop and places the family groups, the source link, the licence, the legal links, the accounts and the version line the org selects for the site; `SiteFooter` gains `groups` to draw only some family groups.
