Templates are grouped by intent. Every template example now carries a `purpose` (Convince, Explain, Show, Act, Operate, Converse, Recover) and a `why` (the user goal its layout serves); `templatePurposes` gives each group its "you come here when…" line, and `registry/index.json` lists the `templates` in that order. The six React Native screens (app shell, sign-in, profile, feed, chat, settings) are templates too.
