Signature components and next-level feedback (DS2, wave W1 of #49) — additions only, the 2.x API is unchanged:

- **New entries.** `./iso` (the isometric kit: `IsoHub` from data, `IsoScene`, `IsoPrism`, `IsoStack`, `IsoDome`, `IsoTile`, `IsoLink`, `IsoNode`, `IsoLabel` — server-rendered SVG, no client JavaScript), `./nav-bar` (floating bar that tightens on scroll, one light gliding between items, accessible menus, mobile panel), `./loader` (`Loader`: delay, minimum duration, ETA, drawn and announced endings; `LoadingRegion`: content ⇄ skeleton hand-over; `useDelayedWait`), `./number-ticker`, `./glow-card`, `./marquee`, `./stepper`, `./meter`, `./chip-link` (a filter that is a link, server-safe `chipVariants`, #45) and `./motion` (`MotionObserver`, `RotatingWord`).
- **Motion (#41).** `MotionObserver` pauses what is off screen (`data-offscreen`), lets the first screen settle (`html[data-motion]`), drives `[data-kz-magnetic]`; `RotatingWord` keeps the width of its longest word (no layout shift); `[data-reveal="soft"]`; icon nodes light up and arrows lean in links and buttons.
- **Button.** `shape="cut"` (45° corners), `loading` draws the Krizaka loader glyph (`indicator={false}` to keep your own), `progress`, `status` success·error (drawn, a ring opens, an error shakes once), `announce`, `shimmer`, `magnetic`; `IconButton` swaps its icon for the glyph.
- **Feedback.** `Spinner` is the Krizaka glyph (a hexagon traced by light around a node) and takes a `value`; `LoaderGlyph` exported. `Progress`: `showLabel`, `eta`, `buffer`, `segments`, `status` paused·error. `Skeleton`: one brand reflection across the page, `SkeletonText`, `SkeletonShape` (the real component as its own skeleton). `Toast`: a hairline counts the time left (paused on hover), `undoToast`, `progressToast`, `groupedToast`.
- **Tabs** glide one light to the active tab in every variant. **Dialog** `placement="left"` (#43), drawers slide from their edge.
- Visual changes (new screenshots): Spinner, Button loading, Skeleton, Tabs, the side dialog's entrance.
