`EngineFlow` config, the first of Krizaka Motion's shared vocabulary: `density` (`sparse` · `normal` · `dense`: photons per conduit), `trigger` (`always` · `hover` · `active`: when they travel), `easing` (`linear`, `ease-in-out` or any CSS easing), `stagger` (ms between conduits) and `breathe` (the engine's mark), next to `speed`, `layout`, `compactLayout`, `particles` and `conduit`. Exports `photonCount`, `FlowDensity`, `FlowTrigger`, `FlowEasing`.
