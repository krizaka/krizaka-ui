Shop looks for owners who want more. `@krizaka/tokens/shop-presets`: three new looks — **Atelier** (an editorial boutique: split hero, service cards, a filmstrip gallery, Young Serif, soft motion), **Rue** (bold and dark-leaning: the TV hero, the poster face, grain, lively motion) and **Velours** (a calm premium salon: a warm surface, the price board, still motion) — each proven AA in both modes for each of its accents like the six others; and `shopOwnerChoices`, the closed lists an owner may choose over a look (title face `serif` · `sans` · `poster`, motion level `calm` · `soft` · `editorial` · `lively`), with `[data-shop-face]` CSS (no new font). `@krizaka/ui/shop`: the document takes `face` and `motion`, and refuses an unknown look, a look version that never shipped, or an accent the look does not list; `ShopFrame` takes `face`; `shopFrameLook(page)` (`@krizaka/ui/shop/look`) gives the frame every look prop at once — the look's own density and motion when the owner chose nothing, an accent only when the look lists it — so a host no longer wires a look by hand; `SHOP_LOOK_ORDER` lists every look (a test fails if a look of the tokens is missing). The composer's look picker offers the title face and the motion level (`setFace`, `setMotion`; new messages `faceChoice`, `faceOwn`, `faceNames`, `motionChoice`, `motionOwn`, `motionNames`).
