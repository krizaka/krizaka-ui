`@krizaka/ui/taped-photo`: `TapedPhoto`, a Cahier block. It is a print taped in the notebook: slightly tilted (`tilt`, eased on a phone), with a white print border, a layered paper shadow, and tape at two corners or at the top (`tape`). It holds any picture, such as an image, a video or a live scene, and takes an optional caption written on the print. The flock's scene that opens www.krizaka.com uses it, and the Polaroids of Akinaza's shop walls build on it (krizaka/akinaza#236).
