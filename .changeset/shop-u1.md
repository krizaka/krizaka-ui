`@krizaka/ui/shop` — a shop's public page as a document (ADR-007, krizaka-ui#742). `shopPageSchema` / `parseShopPage` (zod: sections keyed by id, each a `type` × `variant` with its settings and blocks, at most 16 sections, one hero first, 24 blocks, 12 photos, 6 cards; an invalid document answers every issue with its path), `defineSection`, `migrateShopPage` (pure, schema n → n + 1), the catalogue of the 13 section types with their variants, five starter documents, and the frame and rhythm every section draws in (`ShopFrame`, `ShopSection`: the page frame, one density — `compact` 40 px between sections, `airy` 64 px — a hairline between sections). The first four sections, as server components that receive resolved data: `@krizaka/ui/shop/hero` (`poster`, `split`, `banner`, `tv`), `@krizaka/ui/shop/services` (**`menu`** — the compact price board, the default: a thumbnail only for a service's own photo, a dotted leader, tabular prices, a button that names its service, grouped by category beside the hours — then `cards`, `index`, `carousel`), `@krizaka/ui/shop/booking` (`strip`, `panel`, `sticky`), `@krizaka/ui/shop/gallery` (`mosaic`, `filmstrip`, `wall`, `grid`), and `@krizaka/ui/shop/demo` (two fictional shops with their photos). `CoverHero` takes an art-directed `phone` crop and a `focus`; `ServiceCard` takes `mediaRatio` and its `density="list"` is deprecated in favour of `services · menu` (removed in 4.0.0).
