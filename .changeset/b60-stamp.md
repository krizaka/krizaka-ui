`Stamp` takes the tones `ink` (the page's ink) and `hue` (`--kz-hue-text`), an `icon` slot (a decorative glyph), `size` `sm` | `md` and `straightText`: the border keeps its askew, the text is straight (the default for `size="sm"`). One stamp in the system for the facts, the vibe tag, the prices and the durations of a storefront (krizaka-ui#601).
