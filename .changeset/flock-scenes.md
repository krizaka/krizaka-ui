Flock `scenes`: each bird does a short scene true to its promise — the owl reads a book and turns a leaf, the flamingo and the lovebird talk with a speech mark and their neighbour answers, the crane steps out and bows, the beaver taps a log, the magpie takes a shiny point, the duck builds, the falcon scans — and the pigeon hands its parcel over (it flies back light) while the receiver reacts in its own way. One scene at a time, picked at random with long pauses now and then. CSS-free: Web Animations on a few small elements and timers between scenes, none off screen or under reduced motion. The flock now also waits for `html[data-motion="settling"]` and its stage is a `data-motion-scope` (the page pauses its loops off screen). `Mascot` gets `carrying` (the pigeon's parcel).
