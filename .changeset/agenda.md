`@krizaka/ui/agenda`: `Agenda`, one day's appointments in time order (tabular times, client, service, status chip, a quiet action outside the row's link) with a « now » line between the ones past and the ones to come. The time zone and the locale are props, `now` comes from the server, the open row is `aria-current` and can be an address.
