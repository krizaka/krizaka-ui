Two blocks for product landing pages. `ComparisonTable` (`@krizaka/ui/comparison-table`): a fair “versus” grid — every row aligned across the columns, your column a highlighted band of the same grid, an icon per question, a bold key phrase then the detail, neutral answers elsewhere; one card per question on a phone; rows rise in staggered. `StepShowcase` and `DeviceFrame` (`@krizaka/ui/step-showcase`): a guided tour in one device frame (phone, browser window, macOS window) — the steps beside it with a bar filling under the current one, each recording played muted then the next, crossfading; plays only in view, holds on hover and focus, a play / pause button, no native controls, posters first and the video once seen; dots and swipe on a phone; posters and manual steps under reduced motion.
