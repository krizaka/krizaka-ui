`UpdatePrompt` (the soft invitation to update), `ForceUpdateScreen` (the blocking screen after a `426` or `updateMode: force`) and `MaintenanceScreen` (the service is down on purpose) — web and `/native`, for the app-version contract of `@krizaka/app-config` (krizaka/.github#75). Every word is a prop; the screens decide nothing, the backend's parameters do.
