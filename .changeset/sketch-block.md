`@krizaka/ui/sketch`: `Sketch` and `SketchPair`, a screen drawn as with a pen in a school notebook. Boxes, buttons, scribbled lines, a few words, `@krizaka/icons` glyphs and hatched areas wobble like ink, deterministic from the spec's `seed` (server-rendered, no client JavaScript). Numbered callouts in the margin red point at the items, and a real HTML legend says what each one is for. A callout without a legend line, or the reverse, warns in development, and an unknown icon throws `SketchIconError`. `SketchPair` sets a before and an after side by side from 48rem of room.
