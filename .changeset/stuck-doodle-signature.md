`@krizaka/ui/stuck`, `@krizaka/ui/doodle`, `@krizaka/ui/signature`: cards stuck into the notebook (krizaka-ui#554). `Stuck` sticks any block (a figure, a `SpecimenTag`, a card, a `TapedPhoto`) onto the page: held by `tape` (TapedPhoto's own tape, one strip or `tape-corners`), a `pin`, photo `corner`s or `glue`, tilted from data (`seed`, ±2°) on the Cahier's hard paper shadow. `Doodle` draws an arrow, a circled word, a star, a wavy underline or a tiny bird in the page margin beside a block, decoration only and not displayed below 1360 px. `Signature` is a small hand-signed mark for the end of a page: the Krizaka mark as an ink stamp and « Krizaka · Montréal » in the notebook's pen (`stamp`, `script` or `both`), never a person's signature.
