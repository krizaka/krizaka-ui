A people kit, web and React Native: `Chat` (conversation item, message bubble with its status, composer, typing indicator), `SwipeDeck` with `PersonCard`, `MatchDialog`, `TagSelector`, `StepProgress`, `VerifiedBadge`, `PhotoGrid`; on React Native, `Screen` (header, content that rises with the keyboard, footer) and six templates built on it — app shell, sign-in, profile, feed, chat, settings. Dates, places and sliders come from the controls already published.
