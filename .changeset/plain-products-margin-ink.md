Products wear plain Krizaka UI: under a product brand (`.brand-orazaka`, `-orochia`, `-yasakia`, `-yororis`, `-yomizaka`, `-kachizaka`, `-akinaza`) the margin ink (`--kz-paper-rule-margin`, the red of every eyebrow, folio and margin note) is the brand's accent text colour; the red margin stays on the Krizaka sites. The same scope drops the notebook's faces: headings use the sans (`--kz-font-display` → `--kz-font-sans`, semibold) and the mono is the system monospace (no Young Serif, no Courier Prime). krizaka.com, the blog and ui.krizaka.com keep them.
