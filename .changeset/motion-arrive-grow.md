`motion.css`: arrival and growth, moved out of the Krizaka Motion lab. `data-kz-arrive` on an element or a cell (its table row or list item follows) plays an arrival once — `slide` (default), `fade` or `flash`, chosen by `data-kz-arrival` on an ancestor, `--kz-arrive-duration`; `.kz-grow` around a Sparkline draws its stroke and raises its fill over `--kz-grow-duration` with `--kz-grow-ease`. CSS only, paused off screen, resting done under reduced motion.
