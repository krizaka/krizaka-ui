`Wall layout="tidy"` (krizaka-ui#805, akinaza#477): the tidy Cahier pinboard — a dense CSS grid (3 columns, 2 on a phone, 1 on a very small one), a note may span two, a deterministic tilt of 1 to 2 degrees derived from each item's id, captions clamped to two lines, tilts off under reduced motion. `x` and `y` are now optional (unused by the tidy layout).
