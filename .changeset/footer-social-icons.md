`SiteFooter` draws a social account as its icon (`@krizaka/icons`) inside a link named by the account (« Instagram » for screen readers, icon decorative, 2 px focus ring, colour-only hover), and its `org` mode now reads `org.links.social` of `@krizaka/org` ≥ 1.1.0 like the `config` mode already did (`OrgFacts.links`, optional). An account with no glyph stays a text link. `@krizaka/icons` becomes a dependency.
