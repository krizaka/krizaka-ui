`MediaGrid` can head its grid like its shelf (`grid={{ label: "Videos" }}`, the same heading style as “Shorts”), and both headings take a `hint`: a small “i” that opens the section's one-line definition and a link to the glossary (`SectionHint`, also exported). The section is a named landmark; the hint opens on a press or hover and closes on Escape.
