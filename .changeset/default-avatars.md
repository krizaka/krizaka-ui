Avatar: default profile pictures. `defaultAvatar={{ brand, seed }}` shows the product's own picture (the brand's gradient and a silhouette, one of six tones from the person's id) when there is no photo or it fails to load; `fallbackSrc` does the same with your own image. Web and `/native`. `defaultAvatarUrl`, `defaultAvatarSvg` and the rest of the set are exported from `@krizaka/ui/avatar`; the pictures are served from the Krizaka CDN (`people/defaults/v1/<brand>/`, WebP 256/512 and SVG) and generated by `tools/export-brand.mjs`, which also exports every logo and mark (SVG, PNG, dark and light), avatars and social previews to `brand/<brand>/`.
