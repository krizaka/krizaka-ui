`settings-layout`: a calm, flat settings screen. New `SettingsRow` (label and help on the left, the control or value on the right, stacked on a phone), `SettingsSection variant="flat"` (no card: a title then hairline-divided rows) and `SettingsLayout variant="flat"` (one column of at most 45rem). The section list keeps its hidden scrollbars and `overflow-y-hidden` (tested: no stray scrollbar). The default `card` variant is unchanged.
