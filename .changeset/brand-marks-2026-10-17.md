New marks for Yomizaka, Kachizaka, Akinaza, Yororis and Yasakia (web and native, same geometry), chosen from three directions per brand: Yomizaka "the watch" (the owl, two lenses under a calm brow), Kachizaka "the podium" (gold winner, cobalt steps, a lit diamond), Akinaza "the cut log" (year rings, a 45° copper cut, a copper heart), Yororis "held" (two open hands cup a warm core), Yasakia "one conversation" (one bubble, a tail on each side, the spark inside). The family grammar is kept: dashed orbit and ring, the lit core breathes, everything still under reduced motion, and below 48 px the view box crops to the emblem. Two-colour brands keep their colours apart. The Kachizaka mark's classes move from `kzm-` (Krizaka's) to `kcm-`. `familyMotion` (marks/shared) is the shared web motion.
