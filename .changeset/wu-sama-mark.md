`WuSamaMark`: the founder's byline mark (Oussama's nickname, Wu-sama) — a gold W of five straight legs inside the Krizaka hexagon, split at its top and bottom vertices, ink and one amber, code-drawn SVG, still, cropped to the emblem below 48 px. An original mark, inspired by and not derived from the culture its name nods to: its geometry is measured on the Krizaka hexagon and guarded by tests (no curve, no bat-wing silhouette).
