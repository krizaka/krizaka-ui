# « Le monde de Krizaka : la tournée » / *The World of Krizaka: On Tour*

Treatment and shot list for the epic brand film, the photoreal, much livelier sequel to « Le monde de Krizaka » (44 s,
rendered from code). Brief: Oussama, 2026-10-17. The written reference stays
`~/Documents/krizaka/05_MARKETING_ET_ACQUISITION/marque/REFERENCE-PERSONNAGES-ET-DISCOURS.md`. The cast, the promise lines
and the nomenclature below are copied from it word for word.

- **Length:** ~78 s (16:9 master), 17 shots: 15 generated, 2 taken from the code-rendered film (the opening and the end card).
- **Cuts:** 16:9 (site, YouTube), 9:16 (Reels / Shorts / TikTok), three 15 s social cut-downs (club, dinner, slow-mo rally).
- **Engine:** every generated shot is **Wan 2.2 I2V A14B at 720p** (4-step lightx2v distill, Orazaka worker profile
  `cinema`), conditioned on a **keyframe whose bird comes from the `Mascot` render** (`loops/<bird>-alpha.png`). Then
  SeedVR2 to 1080p, 16 to 30 fps with motion-compensated interpolation, the cut in ffmpeg, and an ACE-Step (MIT) medley through
  Orazaka.
- **Labelled:** « AI-generated » / « Généré par IA » on the picture (end card) and in the file metadata (ADR-080 §5).

## 0. The idea in one line

The flock goes on tour through five eras of pop culture. **Each era is a stage for one promise**, so the gags serve the
promise and not the other way round. The falcon's stand-up bit is about watching, the dinner is about reading and getting
paid, the 90s studio is about building, the beach is about listening and meeting, and the slow-motion rally is about
delivering. We open on the code-rendered film and "switch the lights on": the same birds, now in cinematic photoreal
light.

## 1. Guardrails (checked on every shot)

| Rule | How this film keeps it |
|---|---|
| No real person: no likeness, name, voice or signature look | **No human appears at all.** Audiences, diners and players are birds of the flock or out-of-focus silhouettes of birds. No wardrobe or prop that copies a known person (no braids-and-bandana, no specific tracksuit, no headband-and-mullet, no sleeveless-and-bandana tennis look, no famous glasses or hats). |
| No copyrighted characters, logos, songs, quotes | Generic sets of a **genre and an era**: a brick-wall comedy club, a 1970s family dinner, a 90s home studio, a beach jam, a clay and grass court. No brand on the gear (unbranded sampler, CRT, speakers), no tournament names or court signage, no quoted lines. **No dialogue at all**: the only words are the film's own promise lines, as captions. Music is original ACE-Step, prompted by genre and tempo, never by artist or title. |
| Brand-faithful birds | Every keyframe is built **on the code render** of `Mascot`: silhouette, colours and features kept (falcon: black visor with two cyan eyes, ear tufts, orange-red body; owl: violet, big white eyes; duck: crimson, round glasses, yellow bill; flamingo: pink, long neck, yellow-tipped beak; pigeon: sky-to-azure body, violet wings, the orange parcel; lovebird: green, coral face; crane: pale grey, red crown; magpie: slate, cobalt wings, long tail; beaver: brown, the tooth, the flat tail). "Ultra-realistic" means **feature-film CG realism**: soft fur-like feather texture, subsurface light, real lenses, depth of field, practical light. It never means turning them into real-world species. **At most one era prop per bird, and never on its face** (the falcon's visor stays bare). |
| Promises true to the reference | Each bird does **its own signature gesture**, staged for the era (§2 of the reference). The captions are the exact film lines. |
| Tone | Warm, affectionate, premium. The comedy is in timing, framing and deadpan, and it mocks nobody. All-ages and non-explicit: no alcohol shown being drunk (the dinner has water carafes and grape juice red), no weapons, no gestures of violence. The "mafia" is pure **genre** (sepia, candles, slow nods, an envelope that turns out to be a payment receipt). |
| Brand law §5.3 (*the drawing is never generated by an image model*) | **Exception requested of Oussama** (see §6): the birds are not redrawn. Each one is re-lit and animated from its code render, and any shot where a bird leaves its design is rejected. |

## 3. Sound

- **Music, one ACE-Step medley in six cues**, each made by Orazaka's worker (`acestep-v15-xl-turbo`, MIT) and cut on the
  picture, every bridge on a hit (flash, rimshot, scratch, whip). Prompts by genre only:
  1. prologue: `warm whimsical cinematic underscore, pizzicato strings, celesta, 92 bpm, instrumental` (the film's own bed)
  2. club: `smoky late-night jazz trio, upright bass walking, brushed snare, muted piano chords, 96 bpm, instrumental, ends on a rimshot`
  3. dinner: `1970s Italian-American cinematic waltz, solo mandolin, warm strings, slow and grave, 3/4, 76 bpm, instrumental`
  4. 90s: `1990s East Coast boom-bap instrumental, dusty sampled piano loop, hard kick and snare, vinyl crackle, 90 bpm, no vocals`
  5. beach: `roots reggae one-drop instrumental, warm bass, offbeat guitar skank, organ bubble, 75 bpm, sunny, no vocals`
  6. rally + finale: `epic orchestral slow-motion build, low strings, timpani rolls, choir pads, rising to a warm triumphant major resolve, 84 bpm, instrumental`
- **Foley:** commercially licensed only. No foley model is licensed here (MMAudio is CC-BY-NC, ADR-080 §5), so
  the hits (rimshot, stamp, coin, scratch, clay, chalk puff, crowd hush) are **synthesised in ffmpeg** (noise bursts, sine drops) or
  made by ACE-Step as short percussive stems, never taken from a sample library.
- Mix: music -14 LUFS integrated, hits +3 dB over the bed, cue changes on the cut, 6 ms crossfades.

## 4. Generation plan

- **Keyframes.** For each shot, the bird's code render (`loops/<bird>-alpha.png`, 720², on transparency) is composited onto a
  plate of the set, then re-lit with **FLUX.2 [klein] 4B edit** (Apache-2.0) given the composite as its reference
  image: *"keep the character's exact shape, proportions, colours and face; render it as a feature-film CG character…"*. A
  keyframe is accepted only if it passes the on-model check (§5) against the code render.
- **Shots.** `wan2.2-i2v-a14b`, 1280×720 (16:9) and 720×1280 (9:16 for the shots of the vertical and social cuts), 81 frames,
  seeds `20261017NN` (NN = shot), takes A/B/C = seed, seed+100, seed+200. The distilled model runs at **guidance 1.0**:
  negative prompts have no effect, so every constraint is written **positively** in the prompt (see `PROMPT-KIT.md`).
- **Upscale.** SeedVR2 3B (Apache-2.0) to 1080p, or Real-ESRGAN x2 + lanczos if SeedVR2 is not on the pod.
- **Budget.** ≤ $45 of RunPod (H100 80 GB, $2.7–4/h): ~15 shots × 3 takes × ~2.5 min ≈ 2 h + 9:16 takes + weights fetch
  + upscale ≈ 4–5 h ≈ $15–20.

## 5. Quality gate (frame by frame, every take)

A take is rejected when **any** frame shows: the falcon's visor gone, split or turned into eyes; the owl's eyes not white
discs; the duck without glasses or with a different bill colour; the flamingo's neck broken or doubled; the pigeon without
its parcel when the shot needs it; any bird with **extra wings, legs or beaks, melted wings, a merged body**; a bird turning into a
real-world species (feathers fine, anatomy and face stay the design's); readable text or a logo; a human figure;
flicker or a texture boil over 3 frames. Contact sheets every 8 frames (`epic/review/<shot>-<take>.jpg`), then the
chosen take is scrubbed at full rate.

## 6. Open point for Oussama

The reference's §5.3 says the drawing is *never generated by an image model*. This film keeps the drawing (the keyframes
start from the `Mascot` render and off-model takes are rejected), but the pixels of the moving birds are made by FLUX and
Wan. Publishing it as a brand asset means accepting that exception, and noting it in §6 (history) of the reference.
Otherwise the film stays an internal piece.
