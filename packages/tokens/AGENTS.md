# @krizaka/tokens — Package Contract (agent-neutral)

> Published on npm as **`@krizaka/tokens`**. Lives in `packages/tokens` of the krizaka-ui monorepo: the root
> [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) applies first.

## 1. What belongs here

- The **semantic tokens** `--kz-*` (level 0 — foundations): roles, never palette steps. A token names what a value is
  *for* (`text-secondary`, `scrim`), not what it looks like (`zinc-400`).
- No product word in the roles. The **brand themes** (`src/brands/<id>.brand.json`: krizaka, orazaka, orochia, yasakia, yororis, yomizaka, kachizaka, akinaza — the paused products' four, `PAUSED_BRANDS`, ship with a DEPRECATED header until the next major) set
  the accent family and the section gradient (`BRAND_ROLES` in `scripts/build.mjs`, all of them, nothing else) and
  ship as `brands/<id>.css`, `brands/scoped.css`, `brands` (index, native). [`BRAND.md`](./BRAND.md) is the brand
  system: colours, marks, icons, page language. A product theme imports its brand and overrides only what is its own.

## 2. Rules

- **The source is `src/tokens/*.tokens.json` (DTCG).** `dist/` is generated by `scripts/build.mjs` and never edited
  or committed. `$value` = dark; `$extensions["com.krizaka"].light` = light; no extension = same in both themes.
- **Zero dependency** at build and at runtime: the build is plain Node.
- The CSS keeps three blocks in this order: `:root, .theme-dark` (themed tokens and aliases), `html.light`,
  `:root` (literal invariants). An alias stays in the first block so it re-resolves under `.theme-dark`.
- Every role in `tokens.css` exists in `native` (colours in `themes.*`, `radius`, `typography`, `typeScale`, `motion`
  with `spring` and `duration`, `shadows.<theme>` as React Native `boxShadow` strings, `cut`).
- **The material (BRAND.md §6)** lives in `material.tokens.json`: sheen, fill shade, edges, glass, focus ring, control
  heights (`$extensions["com.krizaka"].compact` → the `[data-density="compact"]` block), the griffe (`cut.shape` of type
  `cornerShape`, `cut.size`). Durations (`duration`, type `duration`, ≤ 200 ms) collapse to 0 ms under
  `prefers-reduced-motion` (a generated block). Its contrast is a test: text on glass over anything the page shows, text
  on the status tints, on-accent down to the shaded bottom of a fill in every brand.
- **Typography.** `font.*` names the three families of `@krizaka/fonts` (display, text = sans, mono), each followed by
  its metric-matched fallback; `type.<step>` is the scale (size px, line height unitless, weight, tracking em, and the
  role in `$extensions["com.krizaka"].role`), exported as `typeScale` (index: raw CSS; native: dp). The fonts are not a
  dependency: an app loads them with `@krizaka/fonts`.
- **The product register (ADR-006, 2027)** is not in this package: the Cahier is the default here (square paper, ADR-004) and
  `@krizaka/ui`'s `blocks.css` sets the calm values on a product's root (`data-surface="product"`), where
  `product-register.test.tsx` measures them. This package carries the four roles the register uses (`text-title`,
  `border-control`, `border-hover`, `shadow-hover`), aliased to the Cahier's own look, and `control.row` /
  `density.{gap,pad}` that `[data-density="compact"]` tightens.
- Contrast is a test: text-primary/secondary ≥ 4.5:1 and text-muted ≥ 3:1 on surface-0..3, on-accent ≥ 4.5:1 on
  accent, in both themes. A value that fails is corrected, never the threshold.

## 3. Quality gates

- `pnpm turbo run check --filter=@krizaka/tokens`: lint and type-check (`checkJs` on the scripts), tests (snapshot,
  parity, contrast), build, size, publint + attw (`esm-only`, `./tokens.css` excluded).
- **Size budgets** (gzip): `tokens.css` ≤ 5.4 kB, a brand's CSS ≤ 0.75 kB, `brands/scoped.css` ≤ 1.5 kB, `.` ≤ 4.5 kB (3.55.0: +0.3 kB for the new roles),
  `./native` ≤ 2.5 kB. (3.55.0: +0.4 kB for the four roles of ADR-006 — `text-title`, `border-control`, `border-hover`, `shadow-hover` — and the row / density roles, in three blocks; the budget is 5.4 kB. 3.36.0: +0.45 kB for the Cahier paper roles — the ruling, the margin, the specimen tag, in three blocks — ADR-004. 3.15.0: +0.3 kB / +0.3 kB for a seventh and eighth brand, Kachizaka and Akinaza. 3.13.0: +0.2 kB / +0.2 kB for a sixth brand, Yomizaka — its accent family dark and light and its mark. 2.9.0: +0.2 kB / +0.15 kB for a fifth brand, Yororis — its accent family dark and light and its mark. 2.4.0: +0.6 kB / +1 kB for the type scale — 14 steps × size, line height, weight, tracking.
  2.5.0: +1.1 kB / +0.35 kB / +0.3 kB for the material of BRAND.md §6 — sheen, edges, glass, three ink shadows in three
  blocks, focus ring, control heights and their compact density, durations and their reduced-motion block, the griffe;
  native gains the shadows as `boxShadow` strings and `cut`.)
- Published surface: exports `.`, `./tokens.css`, `./brands/{krizaka,orazaka,orochia,yasakia,yororis,yomizaka,kachizaka,akinaza,scoped}.css`, `./native`,
  `./package.json`; files `dist`, `README.md`, `BRAND.md`, `LICENSE`.
  Renaming or removing a token, or changing the surface, is a breaking change.

## 4. Release

A changeset for every change to a source (`minor` for a new or changed value, `major` for a removal or rename).
`@krizaka/tokens` is in the `fixed` group with `@krizaka/tailwind` and `@krizaka/ui`: they share one version.
