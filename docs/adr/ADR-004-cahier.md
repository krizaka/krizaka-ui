  `motion.css`, the chrome of the primitives), then every site and app of the family that consumes them

- **Enforced by:** `packages/tokens/test/tokens.test.ts` (« Cahier (ADR-004, krizaka-ui#417) »),

## Context

Oussama showed Krizaka UI to colleagues on 2026-10-20. Their verdict: « too AI, you can tell. It's like all the projects
generated by Claude: same template, same way of doing things, same result at a glance. » The audit measured it on
ui.krizaka.com, www.krizaka.com, dev.orazaka.com and dev.yomizaka.com: Inter + Plus Jakarta Sans + JetBrains Mono (the
default trio of Next.js templates), a centred hero with an uppercase mono eyebrow and two buttons, an uppercase mono
eyebrow above 5 to 6 of every 7 headings, a band of counting figures under every hero, fake browser windows, blurred
glows and washes (up to 11 blurred elements on a page), 231 elements at one 16 px radius on a single page, frosted pill
navigation, lucide-like rounded icons, the fade-up entrance, and the birds used as decoration.

Three directions were rendered on the same three screens (the krizaka.com home with its products, the Button page of
ui.krizaka.com, a Yomizaka lease check), dark and light, desktop and phone: **Cahier** (the field notebook), **Habitat**
(Montréal's modular concrete) and **Affiche** (the Montréal poster wall). Comparison and captures:
`https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/pr-assets/krizaka-ui/identity-417/` (e.g.
[cahier-home-desktop-light](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/pr-assets/krizaka-ui/identity-417/cahier-home-desktop-light.webp),
[the home before](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/pr-assets/krizaka-ui/identity-417/before-www-desktop-dark.webp)).

## Decision

**Cahier**, with Affiche's roots stamp kept for launch moments. The French school copybook meets the naturalist's
plates: the birds stop being mascots and become the specimens the system is organised around.

| Lever | Before | Cahier |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Type | Plus Jakarta Sans 800 · Inter · JetBrains Mono | **Young Serif** (one weight, never bold) · **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** · **Courier Prime** — all SIL OFL 1.1, no Reserved Font Name |
| Colour, light | near-white, black text | **paper** (cool white, never cream) and **blue ink** (`text-primary` hue 212) |
| Colour, dark | near-black `#0c0c0e` | **slate** (green-grey ardoise) and **chalk** |
| Accent | steel blue (ADR-003) | unchanged — the ink of the pen; product colours unchanged |
| Grid | none | the **Seyès ruling** (32 px squares, three 8 px lines) as an opt-in paper (`Page paper="ruled" \| "lines"`, utilities `ruled`, `ruled-lines`), the **red margin** (`margin`) where section numbers hang (`§ 2`) |
| Radius | 6 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 28 | **2 px on controls, 0 on cards, dialogs and sheets**; only the pill stays round; every Tailwind radius reads a token |
| Elevation | blurred multi-layer shadows, glows | **a solid offset of paper** (`4px 5px 0 -2px`), never a blur; `shadow-glow` is a 1 px ink line |
| What floats | frosted glass at 88 %, 24 px blur | **an opaque sheet**: no backdrop blur anywhere in the chrome |
| Surfaces | sheen, light edge, section washes, dome, perspective grid | **matt paper**: no sheen, no light edge, no wash (every brand's section gradient is the page surface), the dome and the grid floor draw nothing |
| Labels | uppercase, tracked, mono overlines | **lower-case notes** in the typewriter face, the margin's red; no tracked caps in the primitives |
| Icons | 1.75 stroke, round caps and joins | **a 1.5 pen stroke, square caps, mitred joins** — a nib, not a rounded marker |
| Motion | fade-up with a blur, an overshooting ease | **ink**: blocks settle in place (no travel, no blur); `data-reveal="ink"` is written left to right; the ease is the pen's `cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.2, 1)`; stamps press with the short spring |
| Navigation | a pill of light under the item | **an ink underline** under the item |

The signature components (the specimen tag, the bird plate, the anatomy plate, the index with page numbers, the
footnote and caption) follow in their own pull request, then the sites, one by one.

## Consequences

- Every consumer changes look on its next bump of `@krizaka/ui` / `@krizaka/tokens` / `@krizaka/tailwind` (one fixed
  group): this is a **minor** by intent — no token is renamed or removed, only values; new roles are added
  (`--kz-paper-*`). A product that needs its previous corner or shadow sets it in its own theme, as a brand override.
- `@krizaka/fonts` 0.3.0 changes the roles; the faces before it stay in the package as `legacyFonts` (files kept, out
  of `fonts.css`) so a pinned `next/font/local` path keeps resolving until the app moves.
- Products keep their own identity (Orochia's Outfit display, each brand's accent); the notebook's ruling is opt-in per
  page, never forced under a dense app screen.
- The Storybook screenshot baselines are regenerated in the same pull request (every story changes).
- Risks, said honestly: the notebook can turn quaint (the ruling stays on canvases, docs and showcases, not under
  dense UIs); the slate dark theme had to be darkened until every product mark kept ≥ 3:1 on surfaces 0–2 (tested).
