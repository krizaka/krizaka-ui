# ADR-008 — Krizaka UI and Krizaka Motion: keep what is used, a functional structure, docs by domain

- **Status:** proposed — 2026-10-28 (Platform design session of [krizaka-ui#837](https://github.com/krizaka/krizaka-ui/issues/837), founder direction)
- **Scope:** `@krizaka/ui` (every entry of `packages/ui/package.json` `exports` and of the registry), the Krizaka
  Motion lab (`krizaka/krizaka-motion`), ui.krizaka.com (`apps/site`), motion.krizaka.com, and the apps that import them
- **Builds on:** ADR-006 §7 (the needs-driven rule, the audit of 2026-10-25, #674 · #675 · #676), ADR-004 (the Cahier is
  for the sites only), ADR-001 (third-party engines behind `@krizaka/*` facades)
- **Data:** [`docs/inventory/ui-usage.md`](../inventory/ui-usage.md) and `ui-usage.json`, generated by
  `tools/usage-inventory.mjs` (tested by `tools/usage-inventory.test.mjs`). Every count below comes from that file.
- **Enforced by (to land with the slices):** the `domain` field of `meta.ts` and the registry test; `usage-inventory.mjs
  --check` in CI; a lint rule in `@krizaka/config` that refuses retired imports; per-domain size budgets.

## Context

On 2026-10-28 the founder wrote:

> « actuellement tout est dedans et c'est beaucoup de composants. La maintenance va être horrible si on ne fait pas ça.
> Focus sur ceux qui nous intéressent et qu'on aime ; structurer krizaka-ui doc aussi, pour qu'il utilise Krizaka UI,
> avec un bon découpage fonctionnel et utile — géolocalisation, formulaire, templates… Même chose pour Krizaka Motion. »

ADR-006 started the reduction on 2026-10-25: 28 entries deprecated (#674, done), 14 merges (#675, open) and the 4.0
removal (#676, open). That audit was a snapshot. It also counted the paused products, which kept their components in
the core.

This ADR does three things:

1. replaces the snapshot with a **script** that anyone can rerun;
2. counts only the **live** consumers;
3. gives the catalogue a **functional structure**. Today it is grouped by twelve UI categories (actions, overlays,
   layout, layouts…), which do not say what a component is for.

### What was measured (2026-10-28)

`pnpm inventory:usage`, against `main` of each consumer:

- **Live products:** Yomizaka, Akinaza, orazaka-web-client.
- **Live sites:** krizaka-com, the blog, ui.krizaka.com's own chrome (`apps/site`; the registry examples are not
  counted), java.krizaka.com (`krizaka-build/site`), the ts site (`krizaka-ts/site`) and the motion lab.
- **Tool:** the internal cockpit. Only its import names are read.
- **Paused, counted apart:** Orochia, Yasakia, Yororis, Kachizaka.

Each row lists:

- the number of files that import the entry, per consumer;
- the names taken from `@krizaka/ui/native`, for the native components;
- which other entries import it (« built on »);
- its size: the gzip of the entry and its chunks, from the published `@krizaka/ui@3.72.0`, against its size-limit row;
- its tests and stories;
- its last change and first commit;
- its category and status;
- the domain this ADR gives it;
- a proposed verdict.

The verdict rules are in `verdict()`; each step is tested:

1. An ADR-006 merge, or a deprecation that names a replacement → **merge**.
2. Already deprecated → **retire**.
3. At least one live importer, the motion lab aside → **keep**.
4. A **kept** entry is built on it → **keep**. This is ADR-006 rule (c), computed to a fixpoint: being built on by a
   retired entry keeps nothing.
5. A filed need recorded in ADR-006 → **keep**.
6. Used only by paused products → **leave-core**.
7. The rest:
   - **ask**, when it is in the Cahier register, used only by the motion lab, or created in the last 10 days and waiting
     for its first consumer;
   - otherwise **retire**.

### 2.3 Retire — 34

- **17 already deprecated (#674).** Removed in 4.0 as planned.
- **17 new.** No live consumer, no filed need, nothing kept built on them:
  - `slot` and `captcha`. ADR-006 kept `captcha` so that krizaka-com would adopt it. It never did: krizaka-com still has
    its own widget.
  - `contact-page`, which ADR-006 already had « on watch ».
  - The unused native-only components: `action-sheet`, `fab`, `image-viewer`, `media-picker`, `onboarding`,
    `otp-input`, `permission-prompt`, `pull-to-refresh`, `splash-handoff`, `swipeable-row`.
  - The native sub-entries `native/vertical-video`, `native/media-capture`, `native/media-picker` and
    `native/markdown`. Yomizaka's and Akinaza's mobile screens import none of them.

  The native components were built for the paused mobile apps (Orochia, Yasakia, Yororis), which pin their version
  (§2.4). Yomizaka and Akinaza do not use them.

### 2.4 The paused products leave the core

**Decision.** A paused product (Orochia, Yasakia, Yororis, Kachizaka) **pins** `@krizaka/ui` to its last 3.x minor
(`~3.<n>.0`) and keeps working as it is. The core stops carrying components for it:

- **The six entries only paused products import are deprecated in 3.x and removed in 4.0:** `iso`, `marquee`,
  `audio-player`, `meta-row`, `how-it-works-page` and `media-grid`, plus `photo-grid`. Each deprecation reason says
  « paused product only ».
- **When a product resumes,** it first upgrades to the current major. A component it still needs then has two ways
  back:
  - into its own repository, as a product composite;
  - back into the core, through the needs-driven rule (ADR-006 §7), from a filed issue.
- **The marks stay in the root entry.** `OrochiaLogo`, `YasakiaLogo`, `YororisLogo` and `KachizakaLogo` are brand, not
  product UI. krizaka.com lists the whole family, and `PausedBrandId` already says so.
- **Krizaka Motion drops its paused-product experiment.** The lab removes `yororis-synthesis` (slice 6).

Why pin and not move the components into a package of their own: nobody would maintain that package. A pin costs
nothing, keeps the paused apps building, and is undone in one line when a product comes back.

## 3. The functional domains

**Decision.** The catalogue is organised by **what a component is for**: twelve domains.

- **The domain is a field of `meta.ts`** (`domain`). It is the single source for the docs navigation, the size budget,
  the owner rule and the guards.
- **The twelve UI categories stay** as a facet: actions, forms, overlays… They describe the shape, not the purpose.

| Domain | What belongs | Examples (kept) | Owner rule |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Foundations** | What every screen is made of: theme, `cn`, motion, router, the base primitives | `theme` `cn` `motion` `router` `button` `badge` `card` `chip` `separator` `skeleton` `spinner` `mascots` `.` (marks) | Changed only with two product consumers in mind, and stable first |
| **Forms** | Entering and validating data | `field` `form` `schema-form` `checkbox` `radio-group` `switch` `slider` `combobox` `date-picker` `search-field` `tag-selector` `filter-bar` | Every field speaks `Field`'s label, error and description contract |
| **Data display** | Reading data | `data-table` `list` `stat` `meter` `description-list` `virtual` `qr-code` `status-chip` `countdown` | Tables and lists sit only on the `@krizaka/*` facades (ADR-001) |
| **Navigation & shell** | Going somewhere and the frame of a screen | `nav-bar` `app-shell` `app-header` `page-header` `tabs` `command` `site-footer` `language-switch` `dashboard-layout` `settings-layout` `native` | One bar for the family (NAV-SYS) |
| **Feedback & overlays** | Telling, confirming, asking | `alert` `toast` `dialog` `popover` `tooltip` `dropdown-menu` `confirm-button` `empty-state` `progress` `upload-queue` | Never colour alone, always reachable from the keyboard |
| **Geolocation & maps** | Places and maps | `map`, `native/map` (Akinaza's explore) | The basemap through `@krizaka/discovery` (org JSON), never a hard-coded tile URL |
| **Media** | Video, sound, images, files | `video-player` `vertical-video` `media-gallery` `document-viewer` `file-upload` `embed-facade` `waveform` `retro-tv` | Media from Bunny through the app's manifest, never committed |
| **Commerce** | Prices, booking, the shop | `money-input` `slot-picker` `agenda` `opening-hours` `reviews` `service-card` `shop` + `shop/*` `shop-composer` `pricing-page` | Money as minor units and a currency, never a float |
| **Identity & people** | Who is there | `avatar` `profile-card` `account-menu` `account-settings` `verified-badge` `notification-preferences` `auth-layout` | Default pictures from the org JSON, never inlined |
| **Conversation & AI** | Talking with people and with the engine | `chat` `genui` `proposal` `analysis-report` `markdown` `engine-flow` `next-actions` | Model output is never trusted as markup |
| **Page blocks & templates** | The sites' pages | `page` `page-hero` `card-grid` `link-card` `argument-card` `cta-panel` `code-block` `toc` `docs-layout` `docs-nav` `landing-page` `docs-hub-page` `legal-page` `error-page` `blocks.css` | One template per page (`page-templates`) |
| **Cahier (sites register)** | ADR-004's paper and ink | `site-cahier` `doodle` `taped-photo` `stuck` `footnote` `chapter` `flock` `mascot-quote` `versus-card` `specimen-tag` | **Sites only**: a product never imports it (§8 guard) |

What the data changed from the founder's list:

- **Conversation & AI is its own domain.** Orazaka's `chat`, `genui` and `proposal` and Yomizaka's `analysis-report`
  form a family that is neither forms nor data display.
- **The Cahier is split from the page blocks.** ADR-004 keeps it out of the products, and a domain makes that rule
  checkable.
- **The UI categories fold into the domains:**
  - « actions » into foundations (`button`, `copy-button`) and feedback (`confirm-button`);
  - « layout » and « layouts » into navigation & shell.
- **Native is a platform, not a domain.** Each native component carries the domain of its web twin, and `./native`
  stays one entry.

## 4. Support levels

The `Status` type of `meta.ts` gains **`experimental`**. The four levels:

| Level | Meaning | Enters when | Leaves when |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `experimental` | It may change or go in any minor. Shown with a badge, outside the main navigation of the docs | It is created for a filed need: its `meta.ts` names the adoption issue | Its first consumer imports it → `beta`. The issue closes without it → `deprecated` |
| `beta` | It may change in a minor, with a changeset | It has its first live importer | Two live consumers (a product, or a product and a site) and 30 days without a breaking change → `stable` |
| `stable` | Its API changes only in a major | It meets the `beta` exit | It is deprecated |
| `deprecated` | It still works, says what replaces it, warns in development, and goes in the major named | A verdict of this ADR, or the rule above | The major removes it |

Today the registry has 22 `stable`, 171 `beta`, 24 `deprecated` and 39 rows with no status (sub-entries and
stylesheets). 74 kept entries already have two or more product consumers. Slice 1 promotes those of them that had no
breaking change in 30 days, and gives the sub-entries their parent's level.

## 5. Size budgets per domain

The per-entry size-limit rows stay the gate (`pnpm size`).

**Decision.** Each domain also gets a **budget**: the sum of its entries' size-limit rows, frozen on the day slice 2
lands. It is a ratchet: it only goes down.

- Retiring an entry lowers its domain's budget by that entry's row.
- A new entry has to fit under its domain's budget. When it does not, something in the domain shrinks, or an ADR
  raises the budget, never silently.

The 2026-10-28 sums of the entries not retired:

| Domain | Entries | Size-limit budgets (kB, gzip) |
| :-- | --: | --: |
| Foundations | 24 | 115.0 |
| Forms | 14 | 203.0 |
| Data display | 11 | 72.0 |
| Navigation & shell | 30 | 227.9 (75 of it is `./native`) |
| Feedback & overlays | 12 | 112.9 |
| Geolocation & maps | 2 | 31.5 |
| Media | 9 | 64.1 |
| Commerce | 30 | 126.5 |
| Identity & people | 9 | 18.4 |
| Conversation & AI | 7 | 39.3 |
| Page blocks & templates | 33 | 125.7 |
| Cahier | 14 | 76.6 |

Three domains measure above the sum of their rows: identity (40.3 kB against 18.4), conversation (74.1 against 39.3)
and foundations' root entry (9.9 against 8.0). This comes from the script's closure measure, which counts shared chunks
in every entry, so it is an upper bound and not a failure. Slice 2 measures them with size-limit itself. Any row that
really fails is fixed there: the code is reduced, the budget is never raised.

## 6. The package layout: one package, domain entry groups

**Decision.** **One package, `@krizaka/ui`, with today's flat subpath entries** (`@krizaka/ui/button`). The domain is
metadata, not a path. The other options were rejected:

| Option | Why not |
| :-- | :-- |
| Several packages (`@krizaka/forms`, `@krizaka/maps`…) | **Churn:** 1,843 importing (file, entry) pairs would change, 1,304 of them in the three products (`ui-usage.json`; `button` alone has 240 importer files). **Version skew:** packages released apart drift. **Singletons:** the router context must be one module in the whole package (`tsup.config.ts`, `krizaka-one-router`), and so must the theme and the motion conductor. Several packages would ship several copies, which breaks them silently. **Releases:** twelve changesets and twelve version PRs per change. |
| Domain paths (`@krizaka/ui/forms/field`) | The same churn for the apps, for no gain in tree-shaking. Each subpath is already its own entry: an app pays only for what it imports (size-limit proves it per entry). |
| **Flat entries + `domain` in `meta.ts`** (chosen) | Zero churn. Tree-shaking unchanged. One release. The domain still drives the docs, the budgets, the owners and the guards. |

**Source folders stay** `packages/ui/src/<entry>/`. Moving them under `src/<domain>/` would rewrite every relative
import and the git history for nothing: the guards read `meta.ts`, not paths.

**One exception is left open:** the Cahier could become its own package (`@krizaka/cahier`) once 4.0 has landed. Only
the five sites use it, so the churn would be small. The question goes back to the founder after 4.0. Until then, the
lint guard (§8) is enough.

**Krizaka Motion stays a lab, not a package.** Its `package.json` declares `exports` (`.`, `./react`, `./core`,
`./native`), but it is private and has **no importer outside the lab** (`ui-usage.md`, last table). The motion that
ships is `@krizaka/ui`'s: `motion`, `motion.css`, `number-ticker`, `MotionObserver`, `reveal`, `PageTransition`. Its
domain is Foundations.

- The lab drops its library build (`vite.config.lib.ts`, `exports`) and its paused-product experiment.
- The lab keeps its guides and canvas studies, grouped by the same domains (§7).
- A motion that proves itself moves into `@krizaka/ui`, as the lab's own rule 1 already says.

## 7. The docs: ui.krizaka.com and motion.krizaka.com, by domain

**Decision.** ui.krizaka.com (`apps/site`) is built with Krizaka UI. Its own chrome already imports 41 entries of
`@krizaka/ui`. Its navigation becomes the domains:

- **The sidebar and the catalogue:** the twelve domains in the §3 order. Each lists its entries, `stable` and `beta`
  first. `experimental` entries carry a badge and are grouped last. `deprecated` entries leave the navigation, but
  their page stays, with the replacement and a codemod link, until the major.
- **One page per kept entry,** generated from the registry, as today. The page shows the domain, the support level, the
  size and its budget, and **who uses it**: the live consumers' counts from `ui-usage.json`. A reader sees what is
  real.
- **The domain pages:** one sentence on what belongs, the owner rule, the budget, and the entries.
- **The docs engine:** Fumadocs stays only as the content engine for the MDX guides, behind the site's own Krizaka UI
  layout (`docs-layout`, `docs-nav`, `toc`, `code-block`). No Fumadocs UI component is visible on a page. Slice 5
  checks this with a test that refuses `fumadocs-ui` imports outside the adapter.
- **motion.krizaka.com** groups its guides by the same domains:
  - forms (validation, steps);
  - navigation & shell (page transitions, view transitions);
  - feedback (toasts, progress);
  - data display (tickers, sparklines);
  - media.

  Each guide links the `@krizaka/ui` entry it animates. The canvas studies sit under « Lab ».

## 8. Deprecation, release and guards

### 8.1 The release plan

| Release | What lands |
| :-- | :-- |
| **3.73** (minor) | Slice 1: `domain` and `experimental` in the registry; `--check` in CI. Nothing breaks |
| **3.74** (minor) | Slice 3: the 17 new retirements, the 6 leave-core entries and `photo-grid` get JSDoc `@deprecated` (with their replacement), `meta.ts` `status: "deprecated"` (`since: "3.74.0"`, `removedIn: "4.0.0"`), the development warning (`src/deprecated.ts`) and one changeset. The paused products pin `~3.73.0` (one-line PRs). The #675 merges keep landing in their own minors |
| **apps** | Slice 7: Yomizaka, Akinaza, orazaka-web-client and krizaka-com move off every deprecated or merged entry they import, through the codemods. `ui-usage.json` shows 0 live importers for every `merge` and `retire` row |
| **4.0** (major, #676) | Removes every `merge`, `retire` and `leave-core` row. It ships `MIGRATION-4.md`, the ast-grep codemods for the mechanical mappings (`rules/codemods/*.yml`: chip-row → `Chip.Group`, quota-meter → `Meter`, step-progress → `Stepper`…) and the size before and after. It is released only when the inventory shows no live importer of a removed entry |

No silent break: every removal has a deprecation of at least one minor, a replacement or a stated reason, and a
codemod wherever the mapping is mechanical.

### 8.2 The guards

1. **The registry test** (`packages/ui/scripts/registry.test.mjs`) fails on an entry without a `domain`, or with a domain outside
   the twelve. It also fails on an `experimental` entry without its adoption issue (`meta.ts` `adoption:
   "<repo>#<n>"`).
2. **`tools/usage-inventory.mjs --check`** (CI, no consumer checkout needed). It reads the committed `ui-usage.json` and
   the registry, and fails on:
   - an entry of `exports` missing from the inventory, unless it is `experimental` with an adoption issue;
   - a `keep` row whose last live importer disappeared on the last refresh, unless it is now deprecated;
   - a domain over its budget.
3. **A weekly refresh** (`.github/workflows/usage-inventory.yml`) shallow-clones the public consumers, reruns the
   inventory and opens a pull request with the app identity when the numbers move. A reviewer sees an entry lose its
   last user.
4. **A lint rule in `@krizaka/config`** (`krizaka/no-retired-ui`), generated from the registry at build. It warns in
   3.x and errors from 4.0 on:
   - any import of a `deprecated` entry, with its replacement in the message;
   - in a product (not a site), any import of a `cahier` entry.

   Every app gets the rule through the shared config. The rule's source list is the registry: no second list to keep
   in sync.

## Consequences

- **The catalogue says what is real.** Every entry has its users, its domain, its level and its budget, from a script,
  every week. « Keep what we use » becomes a check, not a meeting.
- **Maintenance shrinks:** about −31 % of the rows after 4.0. There are twelve domains to own instead of a flat list of
  256. The paused products cost nothing until they resume.
- **The apps change only where an entry they use merges.** That is seven imports today. Domain paths and new packages
  were rejected, so nothing else moves.
- **Cost:** eight sessions, then a short weekly pull request.
- **Risk:** a liked component is retired without anyone noticing. The founder's list (§2.5) and the deprecation minor
  are there to catch it: a deprecated entry still works for one major.
