# Krizaka UI

The catalogue of the Krizaka front-end platform: every primitive of [`@krizaka/ui`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@krizaka/ui),
read through [`@krizaka/tailwind`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@krizaka/tailwind) and the
[`@krizaka/tokens`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@krizaka/tokens) roles, exactly as a product reads them.

- **Theme** (toolbar): dark or light — every story reads correctly in both.
- **Brand** (toolbar): Krizaka, Orochia or Orazaka — a product identity is a set of `--kz-*` tokens, never a fork.
- **Accessibility** panel: axe runs on every story; a violation fails the build.

Source: [krizaka/krizaka-ui](https://github.com/krizaka/krizaka-ui) · Apache-2.0.
