title: Shop presets
description: The looks of a shop page — two per trade, and six that suit any trade — as data in @krizaka/tokens, each proven accessible in both themes.
---

> **A shop picks a look, never a colour.** A preset is data: an accent of the brands, a paper, the roles of the faces, a spacing and a photo treatment. It cannot break contrast because it can only name tokens, and the build proves every combination.

`@krizaka/tokens` ships the presets twice: as CSS (`@krizaka/tokens/shop-presets.css`) scoped by `[data-shop-preset="<id>"]`, in dark and in light, and as data (`shopPresets` from `@krizaka/tokens/shop-presets`) for a studio that lists them. The visitor's dark or light choice is kept; the page never stores a mode.

```tsx
import "@krizaka/tokens/shop-presets.css";
import { ShopFrame } from "@krizaka/ui/shop";

<ShopFrame preset="salon-atelier" density="airy" accent="krizaka" paper="plain">…</ShopFrame>
```

`accent` is optional: the owner may swap the preset's accent for another one **from the short list the preset carries** (`accents`), all tested — never a colour wheel. `paper` and `density` come from the document or the preset.

The six `look-*` presets are not tied to a trade: each also sets the surface, the motion, the mascots, the closing and the page transition, and the photo frame. They are what `@krizaka/ui/shop/look` applies.

No café, restaurant or bar look: Akinaza is for service shops. The accents are those of the live brands (Krizaka, Orazaka, Yomizaka, Akinaza and its copper); a paused product's accent is never offered.

## What is proven

`packages/tokens/test/shop-presets.test.ts` computes, for every preset, in dark and light, on the product register's surfaces 0 to 2 and on the preset's paper, for every accent the preset lists:

- body and small text on the surface: **at least 4.5:1**;
- the accent as text on the surface: **at least 4.5:1**; the label on the accent button, and on its hover: **at least 4.5:1**;
- the accent as a fill, a ring or a decoration: **at least 3:1**;
- the ink on the paper: **at least 7:1**; the hue on the paper: **at least 3:1**.

A combination that fails is removed from the preset's list (the kraft looks do not offer the Krizaka blue in dark, nor Orazaka's orange in light), and a preset that fails does not build. A test also refuses any raw colour in a preset source.

## The photo treatment

The grade (`warm`, `cool`) is a CSS filter on the photo's box, the grain a faint overlay, the radius a token: no photo is re-encoded, and none of it is motion.
