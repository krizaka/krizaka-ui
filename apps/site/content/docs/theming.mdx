---
title: Theming a product
description: How a product wears Krizaka UI — brand scopes, dark and light, and the values a theme may change.
---

A product never forks a component: it changes **values**. Every component reads `var(--kz-*)` tokens, so the identity of
a product is a small set of overrides, applied under a class.

## One scope per brand

`@krizaka/tokens/brands/scoped.css` defines a class for each brand of the family. Put it on the root of the product — or
on a section, to show two brands on one page:

| Class | Product | Identity |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `.brand-krizaka` (default) | The studio and its site | Ink and blue. |
| `.brand-orazaka` | Orazaka | Orange. |
| `.brand-yomizaka` | Yomizaka | Ink indigo. |
| `.brand-akinaza` | Akinaza | Laurentian forest green and warm copper. |

```css title="app/globals.css"
@import "tailwindcss";
@import "@krizaka/tailwind";
@import "@krizaka/tokens/brands/scoped.css";
```

```tsx
<html lang="en" className="brand-orazaka">…</html>
```

## Dark and light

Dark is the default (`:root`); light is `html.light`. The toggle is `ThemeProvider` / `ThemeToggle` of
`@krizaka/ui/theme`, persisted by the app. A component never has a `light:` variant or a literal colour: both modes are
the same component with other values.

## What a product may change

A product theme overrides the accent roles (`--kz-accent`, `--kz-accent-text`, `--kz-brand-gradient-*`), and nothing
else: surfaces, text, borders and radii are the platform's, so contrast stays proven. Components that carry a product's
vocabulary live in its own design system (`@krizaka/<product>-design-system`) and are built on the primitives here —
see [Orazaka UI](https://dev.orazaka.com/docs).

The roles themselves, their values in both modes and their contrasts are on the [tokens](/foundations/tokens) page.
